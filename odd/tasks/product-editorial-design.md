## Design boundaries
- D1-D4 were design-only. I1-I4 implement the chosen direction in Vue under S6. No dependencies, real data/grant changes, existing-service shutdowns, backend activation, deployment or publication. Isolated functional/browser checks are in scope; retired API36221 and credential grants must never be reused.
- Adopt docs/design-system.md and actual --hf-* tokens, not legacy Coco colors or external generated palettes. Self-contained HTML, inline CSS/JS/SVG, system-font fallback only (no remote Manrope fetch). Neutral Mexican Spanish UI.
- Use the same fictional sample: "Arnés ajustable Terra", variants "Mediano · azul" and "Grande · azul". Fictitious operational amounts in MXN; content/photos are illustrative, not live records.
- Distinct operational and editorial scopes. Operational identity/SKU/tax/price/stock do not publish with editorial content. No global Save all promise: existing variant inline saves are separate operations.
- Creation permits minimal identity; images only after product creation. Services do not gain inventory/lots or scheduling. Lot editing stays unavailable where currently unsupported.
- Editorial save != publish; explicit local/ saved/published states, existing subject presentations, family != category, read-only media references. No autosave, public upload, invented variants, collaboration or private warnings in public preview.
- Publication reviews saved draft vs published, preserves permissions/conflict/ambiguous-response distinctions. Care confirmation is explicit and eligibility belongs to server.
- Public-facing preview and families/dictionary are design concepts from the existing brief; label unresolved capability and do not fabricate contracts or policy generation.
- Three alternatives differ in information architecture, not just color. Each shows create/edit plus editorial/review states with functioning local navigation. Responsive light/dark, clear focus, 44px targets, reduced motion, no external resources.
- Chosen artboard: design/product-editorial/03-guided-workspace.html; other artboards remain alternatives. Map visual intent to existing Vue components; never copy prototype state or demo capabilities. Category must not become product/service type. Preserve service, inventory-mode, variant inline-save, image availability and dirty-state behavior.

## Component map and invariants
- ProductDetailView remains the operational state/form owner. Preserve existing schema, handlers, permissions, variant blur/Enter saves, service type and inventory modes. Present identity/presentations with mounted v-show groups; reveal the invalid field's task when validation fails.
- EditorialWorkspace remains the single editor/lifecycle owner. Foundation adds optional embedded:boolean and activeSection:'content'|'review'; no prop defaults change standalone behavior. Embedded uses section/h2, not nested main/h1. v-show switches content/history without replacing controls or requests.
- Operational integration mounts one Workspace outside UForm, only for an existing product and authorized editorial access, lazily on first editorial visit; keep mounted across task changes. No unauthorized editorial reads. Current standalone editorial route stays independent of Product permission/global access.
- Editorial writer uses the stable additive Workspace props for content/review presentation; standalone ProductEditorialView owns its local navigation. Do not change auth/router/store/API/decoder/lifecycle code.
- Publication pending: production Workspace must not expose usable publish/confirmation or promise publication. Keep comparison/history/discard and underlying publisher contract coverage; UI restriction is explicit, not inferred eligibility or fabricated environment flag.
- Existing HF tokens and actual product data; no demo actions, fictitious product illustration, simulated state selector, new uploads or dictionary operations. Summary distinguishes operational completeness from editorial eligibility.
- Two writers launch together only after foundation tests pass; A owns operational view/tests/new ProductWorkspace* presentational components, B owns editorial workspace/publication/view and their dedicated tests. No shared writes or global CSS.
