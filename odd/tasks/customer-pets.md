- S5 Handoff §5–§6 (sale lines): `petId` on `POST /sales/drafts/:id/items` and `PUT /sales/drafts/:id/items/:itemId/pet {petId|null}`; pet must be an ACTIVE pet of the sale's customer (`404 PET_NOT_FOUND`, `422 PET_CUSTOMER_MISMATCH`, `422 PET_ARCHIVED`, `422 SALE_ITEM_NOT_FOUND`); `PUT /sales/drafts/:id/customer` returns `petsCleared` — «Use it to show «Se quitaron las mascotas de las líneas porque cambió el cliente»»; `DELETE …/customer` stays 204 and clears server-side («refetch the draft»). Every line carries `pet: {id,name,species,status} | {id:null,…,status:null} | null`; «In the sale detail and in the history show «Para: <nombre>» from `pet.name`»; printed ticket unchanged.
- S6 Handoff §7: `GET /sales?customerId=<uuid>&petId=<uuid>`; `400 PET_FILTER_REQUIRES_CUSTOMER`, `404 PET_NOT_FOUND` «never an empty list»; «the KPI cards must not change when a pet is selected» — `data`, `pagination`, `filterSummary` narrow; `counts` and `summary` stay customer-wide.
