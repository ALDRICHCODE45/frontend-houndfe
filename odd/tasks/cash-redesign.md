# Caja: rediseño completo (FE)

Redesign of the whole Caja section (registers, opening, shift workspace, movements, counts, verification, handover/custody, closing) with modern UX/UI and accessibility as the priority.

## Specs
- S1 Owner (verbatim): «vamos a hacer un rediseno de toda la seccion de "caja" por favor, no me gusta la ui actual, ten como prioridad la ux/ui moderna, accesibilidad, si quieres puedes investigar en algun lado, haz las propuestas y avanzamos.»
- S2 Proposals first (canvas directions); implementation only after the owner chooses.
- S3 Behaviour, API calls, permissions and money rules (pesos input, cents on the wire, explicit fund confirmation, open-till rule for cash) stay as they are unless the owner decides otherwise.
- S4 Owner chose direction A (L7): «vamos con la A» — design/cash-redesign/01-turno-linea-de-tiempo.html (desktop) and 04-movil-y-estados.html (390 + states) are the visual spec.
- S5 Cortes (L7: «en caso de que no haya, coordinate con el fronted para implementarlos de una forma muy robusta»): the FE adds «Cerrar caja» (POST /cash-sessions/:id/close {notes?}, another person, create:CashClosing, gated by the backend requirements with every 4xx shown in Spanish), the corte detail (GET /cash-closings/:id snapshot) and a «Cortes» list inside Caja (GET /cash-closings, read:CashClosing), PDF GET /cash-closings/:id/pdf fetched with the authenticated client and shown/downloaded as a blob (corte-caja-<folio>.pdf).
- S6 Recipients (L7 item 4): the handover recipient is picked by name and role from GET /cash-handovers/recipients (backend, in progress); no raw UUID input.
- S7 Supervisor lists (L7 item 5): sessions use the new status / drawerOpen filters; closings filter by cashier once the backend fixes cashierUserId.
- S8 Handovers (L8): «Sí, apenas declara (recomendado)» — handover is offered right after the declaration (SUBMITTED), before verification.
- S9 Blind window (L8): «Solo confirmar el último (recomendado)» — while blind, after a cash in/out the cashier sees only the confirmation of that last entry; never a list.
